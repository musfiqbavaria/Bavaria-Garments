EMERALD ROZALIA PROJECT 1 BUILD REPORT

READ THIS FIRST - RECONCILIATION NOTE
=====================================
The build log below was written during the original v2-v32 development and
records what each wave INTENDED to deliver. An independent assessment found that
several of those claims were not met by the delivered code, and some figures in
this file are wrong. Corrections:

  * "Primary pages registered: 70" - data/page_registry.json contains 95. Of
    those, 33 have a dedicated implementation; the remaining 62 render one shared
    placeholder. 20 are legacy entries superseded by a later module and are now
    marked as such.

  * "Forms Master definitions: 650" - correct as a count of titles, but no form
    has a field schema and there is no submit, approve or print workflow. The
    data is generated as 65 departments x 10 categories.

  * "Python syntax compile: PASS" - true, but the project could not start:
    core/settings.py raised AttributeError on import, 30 of 39 templates did not
    compile, and 91 of 95 pages returned HTTP 500. All fixed in Phase 1.

  * "Existing barcode / QR generator: RETAINED" - only QR exists. There is no
    1D/linear barcode generator, although the platform is scanner-driven.

  * Delivery target - the maximum is 15 calendar days (v10), not the 30 stated in
    PROJECT1_MANIFEST.json and page #37. Corrected.

  * v32 Account Master - the page is a static HTML mock-up. No accounting model
    exists: no chart of accounts, journal, ledger, invoice or bill. The same
    applies to POS, retail, franchise and e-commerce.

  * Step 370 print validation - the app is not in INSTALLED_APPS, its URLs are in
    an unused .txt fragment, and the validation logic was never written.

  * Sewing (#42) and Print (#39) have no implementation, leaving two holes in the
    middle of the bundle traceability chain.

Current, verified status is in TECHNICAL_ASSESSMENT.md, which records what was
measured rather than what was intended, and is updated as each phase lands.
